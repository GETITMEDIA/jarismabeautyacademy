Empowering the Next Generation of Beauty Professionals

Jarisma Academy was founded with a simple vision: to offer high-quality, affordable beauty training that changes lives. We believe that beauty is more than skin deep—it’s a career, a craft, and a calling.

We’ve trained students from all walks of life and helped them succeed in salons, spas, bridal studios, and even as independent professionals.

Our Legacy

30 Years of Excellence in Hair & Beauty Education

For over 30 years, Jarisma International Hair & Beauty Academy has been dedicated to empowering women through professional education, practical training and career-focused opportunities in the beauty industry.

Over the past three decades, we have trained generations of aspiring beauty professionals who have gone on to build successful careers across India and internationally.

Our academy provides professional Hair & Beauty education with a strong emphasis on practical skills, industry knowledge and career development.

Our three-decade journey has been built on:

  • Professional Hair & Beauty training
  • Practical, industry-oriented education
  • Recognised certification programs
  • Career-focused skill development
  • Women empowerment through vocational education
  • Strong alumni network
  • Students successfully placed in professional beauty careers
  • Alumni working in international markets

Time to Groom Your Career with Beauty Course

Basic & Advanced Courses in

Hair Styling

Hair styling is the art of manipulating the hair into an desired shape or style. It can be done for personal reasons or as part of a professional service. Common styling products include hair gel, hair spray, mousse, pomade, and hair wax.

Makeup

Makeup is an art form that allows you to express yourself and enhance your features. It’s a way to show the world who you are, and can be a fun and creative outlet. There are no rules, so go out there and experiment!

Skin Care

Skin care is an art. It requires knowledge, skill, and a sense of aesthetics. The best skin care artists are able to bring out the natural beauty of their clients.A good skin care artist is like a good doctor: they know how to treat their patients and make them feel better.

Nail Care

Nail care is an art that requires precision and detail. It is a way to show your personality and style.

Our Mission

To provide industry-standard, skill-based beauty education that combines theory, hands-on practice, and career support—ensuring every student is ready to succeed in the professional world.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create globally skilled Hair & Beauty professionals and connect Indian beauty education with international beauty educators, academies, salons and industry organisations.

Our Certification

Jarisma provides professional certification programs for students pursuing careers in the Hair & Beauty industry.

We also provide Government of India-related certification/recognition, according to the applicable program and affiliation.

We believe certification should be supported by real practical skills, professional discipline and industry exposure.

Our Students

Our students have successfully progressed into careers in salons, beauty businesses and professional beauty establishments.

Many of our alumni have also taken their skills beyond India and built professional careers overseas.

Their success is one of the strongest reflections of our 30-year educational journey.

Our Facilities and Equipment

We use the same tools and technologies found in professional salons, including:

  • Galvanic and high-frequency facial machines
  • Hair spa steamers, dryers, straighteners, and curling tools
  • Airbrush kits, makeup ring lights, and mirrors
  • Nail drills, UV/LED lamps, and full manicure/pedicure stations
  • Salon software for appointment and inventory management
