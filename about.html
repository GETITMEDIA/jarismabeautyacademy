About Us

Empowering the Next Generation of Beauty Professionals

Jarisma Academy was founded with a simple vision: to offer high-quality, affordable beauty training that changes lives. We believe that beauty is more than skin deep—it’s a career, a craft, and a calling.

We’ve trained students from all walks of life and helped them succeed in salons, spas, bridal studios, and even as independent professionals.

The Genesis: Jarisma Beauty Clinic

Founded in 1995 in Pondicherry as Jarisma Beauty Clinic, providing dedicated personal care and professional salon artistry, laying the foundation for 30 years of excellence.

Founded in Pondicherry Professional Salon Artistry 30-Year Heritage
2006
2006 Central Govt. Recognition

Jarisma Beauty Clinic & Education

In 2006, set up a separate service and classroom facility, expanding into Jarisma Beauty Clinic and Education. Obtained Central Government permission to value our certificates, directly benefiting and empowering numerous girls with recognized careers.

Dedicated Classroom Facility Central Govt. Valued Certificates Women Vocational Empowerment

2007 Public Awareness

Beach Road Beauty Competition

In 2007, organized a landmark public beauty competition at Pondicherry Beach Road to create widespread beauty education awareness. The Director of Arts & Culture, Mrs. SUMATHI, was present as the Chief Guest.

Our Purpose & Horizon

Empowering Careers, Elevating Standards

At Jarisma Beauty Academy, every course is driven by a dedication to hands-on artistry, government-recognized credibility, and international aspirations.

MISSION
Core Mission

Our Mission

To provide industry-standard, skill-based beauty education that harmoniously combines scientific theory, intensive hands-on salon practice, and lifelong career guidance—ensuring every student is fully prepared to excel in the competitive beauty industry.

  • Industry-Standard Training: Comprehensive curriculum aligned with real salon requirements.
  • Women Vocational Empowerment: Enabling financial independence with recognized credentials.
  • 100% Practical Readiness: Live model demonstrations, modern tools, and placement support.
Target: Practical Excellence • Career Confidence
VISION
Future Horizon

Our Vision

Our vision is to cultivate globally skilled Hair & Beauty professionals and bridge Indian vocational beauty education with world-renowned international academies, European educators, luxury salons, and pioneering global beauty bodies.

  • Global Artistry Benchmarks: Developing stylists capable of working in premier salons worldwide.
  • Indo-French Linkages: Partnering with French educators and hosting international masterclasses.
  • Innovation & Ethics: Leading the transformation of professional beauty standards in South India.
Aspiration: Global Recognition • Indo-French Collaboration
